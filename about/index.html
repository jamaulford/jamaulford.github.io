Jamaul Ford, known as Jamaul The Maven, is a second-generation entrepreneur based in the New Orleans area. He buys boring businesses and upgrades them with AI, and he founded Brand Maven OS, where we install AI into your business.

His order never changes: diagnose first, recommend second, sell third. Most businesses do not have a traffic problem. They have a bottleneck problem. Find it, fix it with a system, and the growth follows.

Before Brand Maven, Jamaul spent about seven years at East Jefferson Hospital. He holds a master's degree in kinesiology and human performance and has worked in the health industry for 15 years, which led to his second show, Health Forward, on alternative health, peptides, and longevity.
